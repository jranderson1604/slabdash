The Problem We Solve

Card grading shops waste hours every week doing things manually that software should handle.

📅

Manual Status Checking

Shops log into PSA's website one-by-one to check each order. With 50+ orders, this takes hours.

📧

Customer Update Emails

Customers call and email asking "where's my order?" Shops respond manually every time.

📈

No Price Intelligence

Shops have no quick way to look up what graded cards are worth on the open market.

🤖

No Grading Expertise Tool

Customers ask "will this card grade a 10?" and shops guess. No AI tool exists for this market.

What SlabDash Does

One platform replaces 5+ manual processes. Saves shops 5-10 hours per week.

📦

Auto-Track PSA Orders

Connects directly to PSA's system. Status updates happen automatically -- no manual checking.

📱

Customer Self-Service Portal

Customers check their own order status on their phone. Fewer calls and emails for shop owners.

🤖

SAM AI Assistant

AI expert that answers grading questions, scans card photos, and estimates grades + market value.

💰

Price Comparisons & Invoicing

Live eBay + TCG prices. Generate invoices and buyback offers in clicks, not hours.
