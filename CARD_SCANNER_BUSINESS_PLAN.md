## 💰 Pricing Psychology: Why Premium Works

### **1. Anchoring Effect**
Show the expensive tier first:
- ~~$299/month~~ **$49.99/month** (83% off!)
- Makes $49 feel like a steal

### **2. Value-Based Pricing**
Calculate ROI for customers:

**Example: Card shop scanning 1,000 cards/month**
- Manual entry time: 1,000 × 2 min = **33 hours**
- Labor cost at $15/hr = **$500**
- Your scanner: **$49.99**
- **Savings: $450/month**

### **3. Tiered Good-Better-Best**
Most people pick the middle tier:
- Free (anchor - makes Pro look valuable)
- **Pro $14.99** ← Most pick this
- Business $49.99 (makes Pro look affordable)

### **4. Usage-Based Feels Fair**
- Low-volume users: Pay less
- High-volume users: Willing to pay more
- Everyone feels they're getting a deal
